Privacy Policy

IEEE EMBS at UCF · Last updated November 3, 2026

This policy covers the IEEE Engineering in Medicine and Biology Society (EMBS) student chapter at the University of Central Florida's Discord bot, and how it uses the chapter's own Google account.

What the bot accesses

The bot connects to one Google account: the chapter's (ieee.embs.ucf@gmail.com). It never asks members to sign in with Google and never accesses anyone else's Google account. Through the chapter's account it uses Google Drive, Docs and Sheets, and the chapter's Google Calendar.

How it uses that data

  • To answer questions from chapter officers, asked in officer-only Discord channels, it searches and reads the chapter's Drive files, documents and spreadsheets.
  • When an officer asks for a change and then confirms it, it creates, edits, copies, moves, renames, shares or moves to the trash items in the chapter's Drive. Nothing changes without that confirmation.
  • It keeps the chapter calendar and the Discord server's event list in step.

Where the data goes

  • To answer a question quickly, the bot keeps a search index of the text of the chapter's documents on the chapter's own server. Documents removed from the Drive are removed from the index.
  • The parts of documents that a question needs are sent to Anthropic, the provider of the Claude AI model the bot uses, to write the answer.
  • The answer is posted in the officer channel where the question was asked.

The chapter does not sell this data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone else.

The bot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The chapter can remove the bot's access to its Google account at any time from Google Account permissions.

Contact
